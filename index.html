Scope of Practice

Our Services & Process

We design and build residential interiors with architectural discipline. Each service is scoped with documented joinery drawings, material samples, and on-site trade supervision.

01

Full-Home Interiors

Complete turnkey architectural interiors for apartments and private residences. Covers spatial remodeling, civil adjustments, custom woodwork, ceiling coves, electrical reticulation, plumbing fixtures, wall treatments, and fixed cabinetry.

Starting from ₹[1,800] / sq ft Estimate
02

Modular Kitchens & Wardrobes

Tailored modular joinery designed to architectural millimeter tolerances. Moisture-resistant BWR ply carcasses, fluted veneer or matte acrylic shutters, Blum or Hettich soft-close systems, and integrated interior LED channels.

Starting from ₹[2,50,000] Estimate
03

Architectural Lighting & Styling

Technical lighting schemes calculated for lux levels and warm 2700K color temperatures. Specification of solid brass sconces, recessed plaster-in profiles, natural linen window drapery, custom wool rugs, and curated loose furniture.

Starting from ₹[85,000] Estimate
Sequential Methodology

From First Call to Handover

Every project proceeds through five structured stages with signed milestone sign-offs.

Stage 01

Site Study & Brief

Initial consultation, physical laser site survey, structural verification, and lifestyle brief definition.

Timeline: 1 to 2 Weeks Estimate
Stage 02

Concept & Samples

2D spatial layouts, traffic flow analysis, material sample trays (wood veneers, stones, metals), and cost estimates.

Timeline: 2 to 3 Weeks Estimate
Stage 03

Working Drawings

Detailed construction documents, carpentry shop drawings, electrical loop schematics, and plumbing layouts.

Timeline: 3 to 4 Weeks Estimate
Stage 04

On-Site Execution

Civil adjustments, framing, joinery fabrication, fluted cladding installation, veneer polishing, and fixture mounting.

Timeline: 8 to 12 Weeks Estimate
Stage 05

Snagging & Handover

Defect inspection list verification, appliance dry runs, deep post-construction clean, styling, and key handover.

Timeline: 1 Week Estimate
